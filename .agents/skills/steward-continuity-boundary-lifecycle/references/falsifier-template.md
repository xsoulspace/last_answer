# Falsifier Template

Use this compact shape when a self-model, handoff, boundary review, or steward-status claim could become stale, too broad, or over-trusted by a future agent.

Keep the falsifier next to the claim it constrains. Do not move claim templates into evidence docs; evidence notes may reference this shape when they record an actual run.

```markdown
## Falsifier
- Claim constrained:
- Failure mode:
- Check:
- Evidence that would reject or narrow the claim:
- Rerun or review trigger:
- Non-claims:
```

## Fit Checks

- If the check cannot fail, it is reassurance, not a falsifier.
- If the claim can survive any contradictory evidence, narrow the claim.
- If the check needs private memory, hidden reasoning, or unreviewable context, choose a more legible claim.
